Detailed description:
Ever wondered "what is this website built with?" Website Framework Detector tells you in one click — the CMS or website builder, the theme, the full front-end tech stack, and the marketing/analytics tools a site loads. A fast, private, free alternative to heavyweight tools — with no account and no data collection.

✅ DETECTS 17 CMS, BUILDERS & E-COMMERCE PLATFORMS
WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, Squarespace, Joomla, Drupal, Magento/Adobe Commerce, PrestaShop, BigCommerce, Ghost, Blogger, HubSpot CMS, Framer, Craft CMS, Duda — with a real confidence score based on the actual signals found (never a random guess).

✅ TECH STACK CHECKER
React, Preact, Vue, Angular, Svelte, Solid, Qwik, Next.js, Nuxt, Remix, Gatsby, Astro, Hugo, Jekyll, jQuery, Tailwind CSS, Bootstrap, Three.js, GSAP, Stripe, and more.

✅ THEME DETECTION
Reveals the active theme/template where the platform exposes it.

✅ MARKETING & ANALYTICS TAGS
Google Analytics (GA4), Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, HubSpot, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, Intercom, and more.

✅ PAGE INFO & HEALTH CHECKS
Word count, reading time, image alt-text audit, and at-a-glance checks for HTTPS, mobile-friendliness, meta description, canonical tag, social cards, and structured data.

✅ BUILT FOR REAL WORK
• Auto-detects the moment you open it — one click to re-scan
• Copy a summary or export the full result as JSON
• Light & dark mode
• Real brand logos for instant recognition

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs locally in your browser. No account, no servers, no tracking, no data collection. Only the minimal "activeTab" access, and the extension runs only when you click it — never silently in the background.

PERFECT FOR
Web developers, designers, SEO specialists, marketers, agencies, and the simply curious who want to know what platform and technologies power any website.

HOW IT WORKS
1) Open any website  2) Click the icon  3) See the platform, theme, tech stack, and analytics tags.

Open source (GPLv3). Made by Anupam Mondal.
