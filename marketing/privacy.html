Privacy Policy for Website Framework Detector Chrome Extension

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

Created and maintained by Anupam Mondal, a professional web developer specializing in scalable web technologies and privacy-first applications.

What This Extension Does

Website Framework Detector runs only when you click its toolbar icon or press its keyboard shortcut, on the single tab you are currently viewing. When run, it reads publicly visible website content on that page — HTML markup, script tags, meta tags, and response headers — to identify the CMS, site builder, theme, and front-end tech stack the site is built with. This is the same information already visible in the page's source and is read locally in your browser; it is never transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

Information We Do Not Collect

This extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personally identifiable information, health information, financial or payment information, authentication credentials, personal communications, location data, browsing/web history, or user activity (clicks, keystrokes, mouse movement). It has no servers of its own and makes no network requests other than fetching the current page's own headers, which the page itself could already do.

Local Storage

The extension uses your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) only to remember, on your own device: your light/dark theme preference, a private history of up to 25 recently scanned sites (site host, detected platform, and timestamp) so you can review past scans, and a couple of internal flags used to show a one-time review prompt. This data never leaves your device, is never synced to a server, is never seen by the developer, and can be cleared at any time from the extension's history panel or by removing the extension.
